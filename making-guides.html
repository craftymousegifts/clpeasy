A little note about these independent external resources

These are independent external resources, shared for inspiration and learning. The collection is not exhaustive. Inclusion does not mean we have verified every instruction or endorse the creator, provider or their products. Check publication dates, current supplier guidance and the safety requirements relevant to your materials, finished product and where you sell. Recipes may be specific to particular supplies or countries.

US resources are included for making techniques and inspiration; their legal and labelling advice may differ from GB requirements. Explore the original work and develop your own designs.
