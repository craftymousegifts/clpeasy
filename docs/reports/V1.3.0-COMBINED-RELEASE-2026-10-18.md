## Round 2: owner iPhone review corrections (18 Oct)

1. **SDS suitability notice (#257):** the neutral note is now a teal
   information notice (existing `field-alert-info` style, information icon,
   heading "Check your SDS suitability", owner wording, "not" in bold). Text
   contrast on the teal background: heading 5.05:1, body 15.4:1. Separate from
   the "not added" notice. Never amber or red.
2. **Amber warning (#257, option B):** a product name that states a diluted
   form ("(10% in Candle Wax)", "@10% Dilution", "6% in wax") no longer gets
   the amber concentrated-oil box; it gets the neutral notice, which never
   says the SDS is suitable. Neat-oil statements and plain neat-oil names keep
   the amber warning. Real documents: both Nikura 10% SDSs are neutral in both
   copy orders; the 3 neat SDSs are amber when the product name is on the
   "Product:" line. **Limit:** when the product name is copied on a separate
   line from "Product:", a neat SDS gets only the neutral notice (unchanged).
3. **"Not added" notice (#257):** owner's shorter wording; the supplier
   sentence is no longer repeated; codes and support link kept. Exclusion list
   unchanged.
4. **Contact header (#258):** not reproducible. The fixed header (80 px) sits
   above the form at its natural position and after focusing a topic, on
   desktop and iPhone width, with and without `?topic=`. The earlier overlap
   came from the screenshot script scrolling the form to the very top. No
   change.
5. **57 mm recommendation:** supported. Circle custom sizes are 52–150 mm
   (`custom-w` min 52, max 150); the recommender tries the 52/63 mm presets
   and every custom size and returns the smallest that genuinely fits.
   "Use 57 mm" sets a 57 × 57 mm custom circle and the label then fits. No
   change.
