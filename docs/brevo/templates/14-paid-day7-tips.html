Are you getting the most out of CLPeasy, {{contact.FIRSTNAME}}?

You've had CLPeasy for a week — here are three features makers tell me they wish they'd found sooner.

📋

Smart Paste

Instead of filling in hazard data field by field, copy Section 2.2 (Label elements) from your supplier's SDS, paste it into Smart Paste in Step 3 and click Extract hazard data. CLPeasy fills in the information it can identify — check it against your SDS before you continue.
📂

Label library

Every label you save is stored in your label library in the browser you saved it in. When your SDS gets updated, load the saved label, update the hazard data in Step 3, and download — no need to start from scratch.
🖨️

Print Sheet Composer

Once you've saved labels, use the Print Sheet Composer to arrange them on an A4 sheet for home printing or sending to a print company — particularly useful if you're printing multiple scents at once.
