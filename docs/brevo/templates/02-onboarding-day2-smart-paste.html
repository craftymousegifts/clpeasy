The fastest way to fill in your hazard data

Hi {{contact.FIRSTNAME}}, hope you've had a chance to explore the builder!

The part most makers find fiddly is Step 3 — entering the hazard statements, signal word and GHS pictograms from their fragrance supplier's Safety Data Sheet (SDS). CLPeasy has a feature called Smart Paste that makes this much faster.

How to use Smart Paste:

1. Download the SDS for your fragrance oil from your supplier's website

2. Find Section 2 — Hazard Identification

3. Copy and paste that section into the Smart Paste box in Step 3 of the builder

4. CLPeasy will extract the H statements, signal word and pictograms automatically

Not sure where to find your SDS? Most UK fragrance suppliers including Supplies for Candles and NI Candle Supplies list them on their product pages. Always use the SDS that matches your exact fragrance oil and the load percentage you're using.
