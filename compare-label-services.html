What would I pay?

One label design

Speedy: £3 or £6 for a prepared PDF. CLPeasy: buy the £4.99 pack and use one download.

One A4 sheet

CLPeasy: one download, even with different saved fragrances when their dimensions and layout fit. Add your blank sheet and ink or toner.

Eight separate files

CLPeasy: £4.99 pack*. Speedy: £24 standard or £48 same-day, assuming eight separately charged wax-melt PDFs.

One file can be printed repeatedly. EasyCLP’s £0.90 buys 24 physical stickers (3.75p each before delivery); a digital file price does not include stickers.
