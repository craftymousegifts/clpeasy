What's new in CLPeasy

A running record of what's changed in CLPeasy since launch, in plain English.

Printing guides and Q&A 23 October 2026 · Documentation updateView changes
  • Expanded Print your labels with visual guides for single labels, A4 sheets, PDF, PNG, Cricut, SVG checks, mobile files and arranging printing directly with a company.
  • Added 24 searchable printing answers covering printer types, red pictogram borders, label materials, file locations, sizing and troubleshooting.
  • Added printing-help links in Resources, Builder Step 5, Print Sheet Composer, FAQ, Support and the Knowledge Base, plus return links between guides.
  • Updated the Step 2 image and instructions to show grams or millilitres for net quantity. Fragrance percentage remains by weight.
  • Made the registered ® mark consistent in six older page headers, preserving the existing logo artwork.
v1.4.0 — Website and Builder updates Current 22 October 2026View changes
Maker Resources
  • New separate Making Guides and Design Inspiration pages for candles, wax melts, reed diffusers and room sprays.
  • Added matching category images to both resource pages, with clearer navigation, more balanced text and layouts that stack on smaller screens.
A clearer start
  • Refreshed the homepage presentation and free-trial links. “See how it works” now takes you directly to the CLPeasy video walkthrough.
  • Made it clearer before choosing a plan that trial downloads have a watermark and paid-plan downloads remove it. Previously downloaded trial files keep their watermark; reopen a browser-saved label and download again after upgrading.
  • Added a short explanation of CLP’s origins to the homepage and Knowledge Base, with links to official sources.
  • Added a snowy winter header garland. Its warm lights twinkle for 12 seconds after loading, then stay steadily lit; reduced-motion settings keep the lights static.
  • Christmas trees, snowmen and snowflakes fall across the page for 12 seconds. The launch offer stays visible beneath the shared public navigation as you move between pages.
Builder and website fixes
  • Net quantity now has a visible choice of grams (g) or millilitres (ml), alongside the exact-quantity option. Changing the unit does not convert the amount.
  • Removed stray navigation text from affected public pages and corrected an unavailable analytics script address.
v1.3.1 — Support and SDS guidance 19 October 2026View changes
Before using Smart Paste
  • Added a short source confirmation before extraction, with a link to SDS guidance. The confirmation resets when the pasted text, product type or fragrance percentage changes.
  • Clarified that Smart Paste reads one SDS and does not combine safety data sheets or calculate a fragrance blend’s classification.
Practical help for makers
  • Updated Support, FAQ, Knowledge Base and Compliance & Transparency pages to explain what CLPeasy does and what makers need to check.
  • Added guidance on combining fragrance oils, printing and cutting labels, and cutting labels by hand. Cutting-machine information links to official instructions.
  • Clarified regulatory responsibility and Terms wording. CLPeasy helps prepare labels; makers remain responsible for their finished product information.
v1.3.0 18 October 2026View changes
Smart Paste safety correction
  • Smart Paste now reads hazard information only from Section 2.2 (Label elements) of the safety data sheet you paste. Text from other sections, such as the ingredient list in Section 3, is no longer added to your label. (Live since 18 October.)
  • If Smart Paste can't find exactly one Section 2.2, for example when two safety data sheets are pasted together, it stops and asks you to paste Section 2.2 from the safety data sheet for your finished product. Nothing is extracted until you do.
Smart Paste guidance
  • After extracting, Smart Paste guidance now tells apart three cases: a safety data sheet that appears to match your product type and fragrance %, one where this can't be confirmed, and a detected mismatch or concentrated fragrance-oil safety data sheet, which is shown as an amber warning. CLPeasy does not verify or recalculate the classification.
  • If your safety data sheet includes precautionary statements that Smart Paste doesn't add automatically, their codes are now listed once, so you can check whether they are required for your finished product.
  • New "Using more than one fragrance oil?" guidance in Step 3, with a link to the Knowledge Base.
Knowledge Base and support
  • Clearer "Blended Fragrances — What You Need to Know" guidance: a blend is a new mixture, so your label must be based on classification information for your finished product.
  • On the Contact page, you can now choose more than one topic under "What do you need help with?".
v1.2.0 16 October 2026View changes
Label details and fit
  • Choose net weight from a grams dropdown, or enter another exact quantity.
  • When a label needs more space, compare a larger size with tested options for leaving selected details off.
  • Entered details are saved for restoring later. Quantity and batch choices include confirmation that they are shown elsewhere.
  • Candle safety symbols now stay clear of the cut edge on small round labels. If they don't fit, choose a larger size, or leave them off this label and show them on the packaging instead.
A clearer Builder
  • Simplified size warnings, review and export panels, with expandable file-type guidance.
  • Clearer editing, preview and download controls on desktop and mobile.
  • The label preview stays visible while you complete each step, even when the label needs more space.
  • On phones, the batch / lot box is full width and opens the normal keyboard, so codes such as BN-003 can be entered.
