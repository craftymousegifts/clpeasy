What's new in CLPeasy

A running record of what's changed in CLPeasy since launch, in plain English.

v1.1.0 Current 22 September 2026
New and improved
  • Print Sheet Composer — lay out multiple labels on one printable sheet. Choose a ready-made UK sheet template or set your own custom grid, then export a print-ready PDF or individual PNGs for cutting machines like Cricut.
  • Homepage and product previews refreshed for clarity, with more accurate descriptions of what CLPeasy does — and what still needs your own check against your SDS.
Label-building improvements
  • Smart Paste extracts sensitiser and EUH208 warning data from pasted SDS text more reliably.
  • Hazard and precautionary statement text now sizes and fits more consistently across label shapes and sizes, including smaller circular labels.
  • Corrected the signal-word selection logic and added further checks to help it reflect the hazard information entered from the current supplier SDS. As always, verify the signal word against your current supplier SDS before printing.
  • Smart Paste now flags unsupported or unrecognised hazard/precautionary codes instead of silently accepting them.
  • Fixed a layout issue where long chemical or substance names could overflow the label.
Printing and downloads
  • New Print Sheet Composer supports custom label grids and ready-made UK sheet templates, with clear guidance on maximum sizes for cutting-machine software.
  • Cutting-machine printing guidance now appears right inside the download dialog, exactly when you need it.
Usability and accessibility
  • Improved step navigation and responsive layout throughout the label builder, especially on smaller screens.
  • Simplified the hazard confirmation step so it's clearer what's needed before a label can be downloaded.
Reliability and security
  • Strengthened unpaid and trial previews by flattening the displayed label and making simple removal of the preview watermark more difficult.
  • Added a fresh account-status check immediately before downloads to prevent expired trials or cancelled subscriptions from using the normal clean-export flow.
  • General reliability fixes across the label builder workflow.
v1.0.0 15 June 2026 · Original public launch
Launch
  • CLPeasy launched: build GB CLP-style labels for candles, wax melts, reed diffusers and other fragranced maker products.
  • Smart Paste — paste your SDS Section 2.2 text to extract hazard data automatically.
  • Hazard statements, precautionary statements, signal word and GHS pictograms generated from your SDS data.
  • Save labels to your personal library for reuse.
  • Print-ready PNG and PDF label exports.
  • Free 14-day trial, with Easy Start and Easy Pro subscription plans plus top-up download packs.
