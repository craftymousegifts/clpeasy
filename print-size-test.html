Print a size test

Use plain A4 paper. Choose Actual size / 100% and turn off Fit to page.

This free test contains blank outlines only. No download credit is used. It checks size and alignment, not your label information.

After printing, measure with a ruler and check the fit on your packaging. If the size is wrong, check your printer settings before printing labels.
