Do I need a CLP label? What makers in Great Britain need to know

Selling fragrance products? Check your finished product under GB CLP

⚖️
What is a CLP label?
Before selling a fragrance product in Great Britain, assess the finished formulation using the current supplier SDS and applicable CLP information. If the product is classified as hazardous or supplemental labelling requirements apply, it must carry the required GB CLP label.
🏷️
What does it include?
Depending on your finished product, a label may need hazard symbols, warning text and supplier details. CLPeasy extracts label information from suitable SDS documentation for you to review and turn into a print-ready label.
🚨
What happens if you don't have one?
Where CLP labelling is legally required, missing or incorrect hazard information may lead to enforcement action, including product withdrawal. Makers must verify their finished labels against the current supplier SDS and applicable official guidance before sale.
CLPeasy is currently available for makers selling in England, Scotland and Wales.

Where did CLP come from?

CLP is based on the United Nations’ Globally Harmonised System (GHS), which helps communicate chemical hazards consistently. The EU CLP Regulation entered into force in 2009, with 1 June 2015 as the main deadline for mixtures to use the system. Since 1 January 2021, Great Britain has followed GB CLP.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a CLP label and do I legally need one?
If you sell a candle, wax melt or another fragrance product in Great Britain, CLP labelling requirements may apply depending on the product's classification. The finished label may need hazard pictograms, a signal word, hazard and precautionary statements, sensitiser information and responsible-person details. Always verify the finished label against the current supplier SDS and applicable official GB CLP guidance before sale.
Will my labels be legally compliant?
CLPeasy helps you create CLP Ready labels from suitable SDS documentation. It does not classify your finished product or certify compliance. Check that the source information covers your formulation and review the exported label before use. Read our Compliance & Transparency page.
What is an SDS sheet and where do I find mine?
An SDS (Safety Data Sheet) is a document provided by your fragrance supplier that contains all the hazard information for their fragrance oil. You should find it on your supplier's website — usually in a dedicated SDS or safety section. Smart Paste accepts either the complete SDS or Section 2.2 (Label elements). Use the current SDS that applies to your product, concentration and target market. CLPeasy extracts the label information it can identify; check every extracted item against your supplier's Section 2.2 before continuing. If you cannot find the correct SDS, ask your supplier for the current document applicable to your product, concentration and target market.
How does Smart Paste work?
Open your current supplier SDS PDF and paste either the complete SDS or the complete Section 2.2 (Label elements) into Smart Paste. CLPeasy extracts the label information it can identify; review every item against the supplier's Section 2.2 before continuing.
My supplier isn't in the library — can I still use CLPeasy?
Copy Section 2.2 (Label elements) from your current supplier SDS into Smart Paste. CLPeasy extracts the available hazard information for you to review against the source document. Supplier formats vary, and the SDS must be applicable to your product, concentration and target market.
What size labels does CLPeasy generate?
CLPeasy supports three shapes — circle, square and rectangle. Choose your shape, then enter the exact width and height you need in millimetres, from wax melt clamshells and small tins up to large jar candles. CLPeasy currently enforces a 52mm minimum for standalone labels as a product safeguard. This is a CLPeasy minimum, not a universal GB CLP legal label-size rule; the space needed depends on the required information and applicable rules.
How do I use my label in Cricut Design Space?
Use a compatible Cricut Print Then Cut workflow: a separate printer prints your design, then your Cricut cuts it. Keep the label at its intended dimensions. See our practical printing and cutting guides for the steps.
Does CLPeasy work on mobile?
Yes — CLPeasy is fully mobile friendly and works on iPhone and Android. You can build, preview and download labels directly from your phone. PNG files save to your camera roll and SVG files save to your files app.
What is EUH208 and do I need it on my label?
EUH208 is a supplementary hazard statement that means "Contains [substance name] — may produce an allergic reaction." You need it on your label if your fragrance contains a CLP/REACH classified skin sensitiser above the threshold concentration (0.01% for category 1A, 0.1% for category 1B). Your SDS Section 2.2 will tell you exactly which sensitisers are present and whether EUH208 applies. CLPeasy has a built-in library of all 28 common CLP fragrance sensitisers to help you.
