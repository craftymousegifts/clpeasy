Compliance & Regulation

No. Contact with the HSE Helpdesk does not constitute approval or certification of CLPeasy, its labels or customers’ products. Read our Compliance & Transparency page for the correspondence summary.

HSE describes manufacturers, importers, downstream users and distributors that place chemicals on the Great Britain market as suppliers, with duties depending on their role in the supply chain. Makers who formulate mixtures can be downstream users and have duties to classify, label and package those mixtures before placing them on the GB market.

CLPeasy supports the practical label-building process by extracting, organising, checking and formatting the information you provide. You still need to review the finished label and make sure the source information and classification are appropriate for the product you place on the market.

Finished cosmetic products intended for the final user are outside GB CLP scope.

If a bath bomb, soap or other product is a cosmetic product in its finished state intended for the final user, HSE states that GB CLP does not apply to that finished cosmetic. Cosmetic products are instead subject to the applicable cosmetics rules.

CLPeasy is not a cosmetic-labelling tool.

CLPeasy is designed for GB CLP hazard labels for products within GB CLP scope. If your product is a cosmetic, use the applicable cosmetics requirements and OPSS guidance rather than relying on a CLPeasy CLP label for the finished cosmetic.

If you are unsure, consult the OPSS guidance on cosmetic products or speak with a qualified cosmetic safety assessor.

Using CLPeasy

Safety Data Sheets are required under UK REACH in specified circumstances, including for hazardous chemicals supplied for use at work and some mixtures containing hazardous substances. Where an SDS is supplied, Section 2.2 contains the label elements for that supplied chemical or mixture.

For CLPeasy, use the current supplier information that is applicable to your finished product and fragrance load. Smart Paste lets you paste the relevant Section 2.2 text and extracts available label information for you to review.

Yes. CLPeasy lets you save labels to your browser-based library and reload them on the same device. This means once you have set up a product, generating a new label or reprinting an existing one takes seconds.

We recommend reviewing your saved labels whenever your fragrance supplier updates their SDS, as hazard classifications can change between batches or supplier reformulations.

You need appropriate information for the whole finished formulation. CLPeasy cannot combine SDSs or calculate a custom blend's classification. Read Combining fragrance oils: where to start before preparing the label.

Use current SDS documentation confirmed as applicable to your product type, formulation, concentration and the GB market. An SDS for concentrated oil or a different product base is not automatically suitable. Smart Paste reads the complete Section 2.2.

Do not assume a higher-concentration SDS is automatically appropriate. Ask the document provider to confirm whether it covers your actual finished formulation. CLPeasy does not calculate or validate a different concentration for you.
