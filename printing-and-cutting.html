Printing and cutting your CLPeasy labels

You can print and cut small batches by hand, or use a compatible Cricut Print Then Cut setup. Choose the method that suits your equipment.

  • Print at home and cut by hand — no cutting machine required.
  • Use Cricut Design Space Print Then Cut — your printer prints; your Cricut cuts.

These guides cover practical printing and cutting. For classification and labelling responsibilities, visit Compliance & Transparency.
