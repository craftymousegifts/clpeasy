About CLPeasy

What is CLPeasy?

CLPeasy is a tool that makes creating CLP-compliant labels simple for candle and wax melt makers. Instead of manually working out hazard statements, pictograms and signal words, CLPeasy does the hard work for you — just enter your product and fragrance details and download a print-ready label.

You're among the very first people to use it. Your feedback will directly shape the product before we launch to the public.

Your Trial

Your 7-day free access

You have full access to CLPeasy for 7 days from the date your account is activated. There is no payment required and no card details needed — ever.

At the end of your 7 days, your access will end and you'll be shown a friendly message. We'll be in touch before then to collect your feedback.

⚠️ Important: CLP labels generated using CLPeasy are based on the SDS data provided by your fragrance supplier. You remain responsible for ensuring your labels are accurate and compliant for your products. CLPeasy is a tool to assist, not a substitute for your own due diligence.
Getting Started

How to sign up and get started

1
Go to clpeasy.com Visit clpeasy.com/auth.html on your phone or desktop.
2
Create your account Click Sign Up and use the same email address you applied with. Set a password you'll remember.
3
Verify your email You'll receive a verification email from noreply@clpeasy.com. Click the link inside to confirm your account. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
4
Email us to confirm Once you've signed up, please email support@clpeasy.com to let us know you're in. We'll then activate your 7-day access.
5
Start making labels! Head to the builder and create your first label. The 5-step process takes just a few minutes.
What to Test

Please try all of these during your 7 days

  • ✓ Create at least 2–3 labels using your real products and fragrances
  • ✓ Try the Smart Paste feature with an SDS sheet from your fragrance supplier
  • ✓ Test different label shapes and sizes — circle, square, and rectangle
  • ✓ Download a label in PNG format and check how it looks when printed
  • ✓ Try the builder on both mobile and desktop if you can
  • ✓ Save a label to your library and reload the page to check it persists
  • ✓ Notice anything that confuses you, looks wrong, or could be improved
