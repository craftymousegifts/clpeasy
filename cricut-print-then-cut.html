Use Cricut Design Space Print Then Cut

Print Then Cut uses a separate printer and a compatible Cricut cutting machine. These steps describe the general workflow; follow Cricut's current instructions for your device and machine.

  1. Download your reviewed label from CLPeasy and upload the SVG into Design Space.
  2. Check its width and height in millimetres against your CLPeasy label. Keep the intended size.
  3. Make the complete label printable. For a layered SVG, select the artwork together with its full label background and Flatten it into a Print Then Cut layer. Check the preview shows the intended outside edge.
  4. Choose a supported sheet size. If it will not fit, reduce the number of labels or change to a supported larger sheet rather than shrinking the label.
  5. Follow Design Space's printing prompts. It creates the sensor marks your Cricut needs. Keep printing and cutting in the same Design Space workflow, without changing page scale.
  6. Test one label and measure it before a batch. Check that the cut follows the outside edge and preserves all content; follow Cricut's calibration guidance if alignment is inaccurate.

For full instructions, supported equipment, materials and troubleshooting, use Cricut's official Print Then Cut guide.
