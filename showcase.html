CLP Compliance

What's on every CLPeasy label

Every element below is required under UK CLP Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008. CLPeasy generates them automatically from your SDS Section 2.2 data — you never have to look anything up manually.

1
Product name & scent
Your scent name and product type (candle, wax melt, diffuser etc.) displayed prominently on every label
2
GHS pictograms
The correct hazard diamond symbols auto-selected from your SDS Section 2.2 data — exclamation, environment, health hazard and more
3
Signal word
WARNING or DANGER — automatically determined from your SDS data. CLPeasy will never apply the wrong signal word
4
H statements
Hazard statements in full — H317, H411, H315 and others extracted from your SDS and printed in the correct UK CLP format
5
P statements
Precautionary statements — P102, P273, P501 and others. Select from the full library or let Smart Paste extract them from your SDS
6
Sensitiser declaration
EUH208 "Contains [allergen]" added automatically when your SDS requires it — essential for fragrances containing skin sensitisers
7
UFI code
Unique Formula Identifier auto-generated for every label — required for EU Poison Centre Notification and increasingly expected on UK products
8
Responsible person
Your business name, address and contact details — the legal responsible person under UK CLP must appear on every label
9
Net quantity & batch number
Net weight or volume and an optional batch reference — required for traceability and consumer information under UK product law
QR
QR digital safety sheet
Every label includes a QR code linking to a permanent digital safety sheet — stays live even if your subscription lapses, so products already sold are always covered
📋 UK CLP Regulation: All CLPeasy labels comply with UK CLP Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, retained in UK law after EU exit. Every required element is included and cannot be omitted. You remain responsible for verifying hazard data against your actual SDS at your specific fragrance load before printing.
