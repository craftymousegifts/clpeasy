Reed Diffuser CLP labels for makers in Great Britain

Preparing reed diffusers for sale in Great Britain? See how fragrance, base liquid and finished-mixture information affect GB CLP label preparation.

Scope: This is introductory guidance for Great Britain only, not Northern Ireland or the EU. CLPeasy creates label artwork from suitable information; it does not classify finished mixtures or guarantee legal compliance. The maker or supplier remains responsible for checking their finished product.

Base liquids, fragrance and the finished mixture

A reed diffuser commonly contains a base liquid and one or more fragrance components. The hazard classification of a finished diffuser mixture may differ from the classification of either individual ingredient. CLPeasy does not calculate the classification of a blend from multiple SDS documents.

Before creating your label

  1. Identify the exact diffuser base and fragrance ingredients, with their current supplier SDS documents.
  2. Obtain or establish the classification and required labelling information for the finished diffuser mixture.
  3. Check any required hazard pictograms, statements and supplier details before using the Builder.
  4. Prepare and review a label, then check the print against the actual bottle and packaging.

Remember: If you combine ingredients and do not have suitable finished-mixture classification information, seek competent advice before relying on label artwork.
