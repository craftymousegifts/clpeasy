No — a UFI (Unique Formula Identifier) is not currently a legal requirement for products sold only in Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales). It is required for products sold in the EU and in Northern Ireland, which follows EU CLP regulation under the Northern Ireland Protocol.

However, if you sell online — on Etsy, Not On The High Street, your own website, or anywhere that could reach EU or NI customers — it is best practice to include a UFI on all your labels. It costs nothing to generate one in CLPeasy, and it means you're covered if your reach extends beyond GB.

Yes — UFIs are required across all EU member states and Northern Ireland for any mixture classified as hazardous under CLP regulation. This includes all 27 EU countries: France, Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Portugal, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Ireland, and the rest.

The UFI links your product to a dossier submitted to national poison centres (in the UK, that's the National Poisons Information Service). In a medical emergency, this code allows healthcare professionals to identify exactly what is in your product and respond appropriately. This is why it's a legal requirement — it's a safety mechanism, not just an admin exercise.

Outside the EU, requirements vary. Australia, Canada and the US have their own chemical labelling systems and do not currently require a UFI in the CLP sense — but if you are selling internationally, always check the specific regulations for each market.
