New scents = new labels

Every time you add a new fragrance to your range, you may need a new or updated CLP label based on that product's classification and current supplier information. CLPeasy helps you build and update the label without starting the layout from scratch.

Formula changes = updated labels

Changed your fragrance load percentage? Switched to a different supplier for the same scent? The hazard classification can change — which means your label must change too. Selling an outdated label is a legal risk.

🎨

Rebranding = new labels across your range

New logo, new colour, new business name or address? Every label in your entire range needs to be regenerated. With CLPeasy™ your details are saved — change once and regenerate everything in minutes.

Seasonal ranges = regular label runs

Christmas, Valentine’s, Mother’s Day, autumn collections — seasonal makers are constantly building new scents and retiring old ones. Your label needs keep pace with your creativity.

Adding reed diffusers to your candle range? Launching a room spray? Each product type has different CLP requirements. CLPeasy™ handles them all — candles, wax melts, diffusers, room sprays and more.
