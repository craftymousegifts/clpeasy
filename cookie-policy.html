Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026  ·  Effective: 26 May 2026

This policy explains what cookies CLPeasy uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. We comply with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — such as whether you are logged in — making the experience smoother and more personalised.

2. How We Use Cookies

CLPeasy uses a minimal set of cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share cookie data with advertisers.

3. Cookies We Use

CookieTypePurposeDuration
sb-access-token Essential Keeps you logged in to your CLPeasy account (set by Supabase authentication) 1 hour (refreshed automatically)
sb-refresh-token Essential Refreshes your login session so you stay logged in (set by Supabase) 60 days
localStorage (clpeasy-*) Functional Saves your business details and label preferences locally in your browser so you don't have to retype them each visit. This is browser storage, not a cookie, but works similarly. Until you clear your browser data

4. Essential Cookies

The authentication cookies listed above are strictly necessary for CLPeasy to function. Without them, you would not be able to log in or save your labels. Under PECR, strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent.

5. Third-Party Cookies

When you choose to pay for a Pro subscription, you will be redirected to Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe may set its own cookies on that page. Please refer to Stripe's Privacy Policy for details.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google may set cookies as part of their OAuth authentication flow. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

CLPeasy does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to CLPeasy.

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Continued use of CLPeasy after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at support@clpeasy.com.
