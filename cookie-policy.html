Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026  ·  Effective: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what cookies CLPeasy uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. We comply with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — such as whether you are logged in — making the experience smoother and more personalised.

2. How We Use Cookies

CLPeasy itself does not set cookies. To keep you signed in and to remember your labels and settings, it uses your browser's local storage, which works in a similar way to cookies and is listed below. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share cookie data with advertisers.

4. Essential Cookies

The storage listed above is strictly necessary for CLPeasy to function. Without it, you would not be able to sign in or save your labels. Under PECR, strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent.

5. Third-Party Cookies

When you choose to buy Pay As You Go downloads or an Easy Start Unlimited subscription, you will be redirected to Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe may set its own cookies on that page. Please refer to Stripe's Privacy Policy for details.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google may set cookies as part of their OAuth authentication flow. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

CLPeasy does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other advertising or analytics tracking cookies. We use Plausible Analytics to count page visits and produce aggregated statistics that help us improve CLPeasy. Plausible does not set cookies. Its script does read one setting from your browser's local storage (plausible_ignore), which is how a visitor can tell it not to count their visits.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to CLPeasy.
