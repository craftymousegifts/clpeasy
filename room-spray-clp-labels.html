Room Spray CLP labels for makers in Great Britain

Selling room sprays in Great Britain? Learn what to check before creating GB CLP label artwork for a fragrance-based spray mixture.

Scope: This is introductory guidance for Great Britain only, not Northern Ireland or the EU. CLPeasy creates label artwork from suitable information; it does not classify finished mixtures or guarantee legal compliance. The maker or supplier remains responsible for checking their finished product.

Spray formulations and suitable SDS information

Room spray formulations may include solvents, water, fragrance and other ingredients. Flammability and other hazards depend on the finished formulation, not the product name. The supplier SDS for a fragrance ingredient should not automatically be treated as an SDS for the finished room spray.

Before creating your label

  1. Document the finished room spray formulation and collect current supplier safety information.
  2. Establish the finished mixture's GB CLP classification and the label information that applies.
  3. Review the relevant hazards, wording and packaging space before preparing artwork.
  4. Use the Builder with suitable information and verify the finished printed label.

Remember: Spray packaging and other product-safety considerations may require separate checks beyond the scope of CLPeasy's label artwork tool.
