🛠️ Support

Real people. Real support. Here to help.

New to CLPeasy, or stuck on your next label? You're welcome to get in touch. I'm Michaela, the maker behind CLPeasy, and I respond to support enquiries personally.

You don't need to know the technical terms. Tell me what you're trying to do and where you're stuck, and I'll help you with the next step in using CLPeasy.

Before you contact us

For a display or download problem, try refreshing the page and checking that you're signed into the correct account. Include your device, browser and any error message when you contact us. You can contact us even if you haven't tried these checks.

Saved labels are stored in your browser. A private window or a different browser won't show that saved library. Please don't clear your browser's site data as a troubleshooting step without first preserving anything you need.

Something missing after Smart Paste?

Use the complete Section 2.2 (Label elements) from the current SDS applicable to your product, and compare the extracted result with that section. If anything is missing or different, pause before using the label and contact support. The Builder does not provide manual hazard entry or overrides.

Tell us what you need

Tell us what you're trying to do, what happened, and any message shown on screen. If your question is about extraction, include the SDS title, supplier, revision date and relevant Section 2.2 text, or a link to the document if available.

Please don't include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive account information.

Use the form below or email support@clpeasy.com. We aim to reply as soon as possible.
