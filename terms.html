Clause 2

The Service

CLPeasy is a web-based software tool designed to assist UK makers of candles, wax melts, reed diffusers, and other fragranced products with preparing hazard labels using information from their supplier's SDS.

CLPeasy operates as follows:

  • Users enter their product details, fragrance load percentage, and hazard classification data sourced from their fragrance supplier's Safety Data Sheet (Section 2.2)
  • CLPeasy formats the hazard statements, precautionary statements, pictograms, signal word, allergen Contains line and responsible-person details supplied by the user
  • CLPeasy does not independently classify substances — it assists users in applying classifications already provided by their fragrance supplier

Clause 5

Cosmetic Products Notice

CLPeasy is designed to assist with CLP hazard labelling requirements. Certain product types — including but not limited to bath bombs, soaps, body lotions, lip balms, and other products applied to the body — are additionally governed by UK Cosmetics Regulation.

Important: If you make cosmetic products, you may be legally required to have a Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) completed by a qualified cosmetic safety assessor, notify your product on the SCPN portal, and include additional mandatory label elements (INCI list, responsible person, batch number, etc.) under UK Cosmetics Regulation. CLPeasy does not cover these cosmetic labelling requirements. Using CLPeasy alone does not make a cosmetic product compliant with UK Cosmetics Regulation.

For guidance on cosmetic product compliance, refer to the OPSS cosmetic products guidance.
