Clause 2

The Service

CLPeasy is a web-based software tool designed to assist UK makers of candles, wax melts, reed diffusers, and other fragranced products with preparing hazard labels using information from their supplier's SDS.

CLPeasy operates as follows:

  • Users enter their product details, fragrance load percentage where relevant (optional), and hazard classification data sourced from their fragrance supplier's Safety Data Sheet (Section 2.2)
  • CLPeasy formats the hazard statements, precautionary statements, pictograms, signal word, allergen Contains line and responsible-person details supplied by the user
  • CLPeasy does not independently classify substances — it assists users in applying classifications already provided by their fragrance supplier

Clause 5

Cosmetic Products Notice

CLPeasy is designed to assist with GB CLP hazard labelling for products within the scope of GB CLP. HSE states that GB CLP does not apply to cosmetic products in the finished state intended for the final user.

Important: Finished cosmetic products are subject to separate cosmetics requirements. CLPeasy is not a cosmetic-labelling or cosmetic-safety-assessment tool. If you make cosmetic products, use the applicable OPSS cosmetics guidance and do not rely on a CLPeasy GB CLP label as a substitute for the requirements that apply to the finished cosmetic.

For guidance on cosmetic product compliance, refer to the OPSS cosmetic products guidance.
