# M23 — more than six precautionary statements — GB CLP investigation

Status: INVESTIGATED — GUIDANCE/SELECTION ISSUE, NOT A BLIND HARD LIMIT

## GB-only regulatory finding

GB CLP Article 28 sets principles of precedence for precautionary statements.

It requires redundant or unnecessary precautionary statements to be omitted, and states that not more than six precautionary statements should appear on the label **unless necessary to reflect the nature and severity of the hazards**.

Therefore "six" is not an unconditional technical maximum.

## CLPeasy implication

CLPeasy should not simply reject a seventh P statement in all cases.

Nor should it encourage makers to retain a long unreviewed list merely because every code was found in an SDS.

The appropriate product behaviour is to make the maker aware when more than six are selected and direct them to the supplier's current Section 2 label elements / the GB precedence-selection requirement, while preserving genuinely necessary statements.

Any automatic removal must be based on verified GB precedence/selection logic, not an arbitrary first-six truncation.
